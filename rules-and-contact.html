RULE 02

₹50 Refundable Security Escrow Policy (100% Refundable)

To prevent bot spam, automated vandalism, and duplicate prank complaints that exhaust civic field teams, a refundable token deposit of ₹50 is temporarily reserved in municipal escrow when filing a complaint:

  • 100% Automatic Refund: The entire ₹50 deposit is instantly credited back to your Civica Wallet as soon as the assigned municipal engineer verifies the photo and geo-location on site.
  • Zero Cost for Genuine Citizens: No charges or fees are deducted for any legitimate complaint, regardless of how long repairs take.
  • Instant Wallet Payout: Refunded wallet funds can be reused for future reports, BRTS transit rides, or withdrawn to UPI/Bank account.
  • Malicious Spam Forfeiture: The deposit is only forfeited in cases of verified deliberate hoax photos or abusive spam submissions to compensate inspection transit costs.
💡 Citizen Guarantee: Over 99.4% of all citizen escrow deposits are refunded within 24 hours. Your civic participation is safe and protected.
RULE 03

Evidence & Geo-Tagging Standards (Live GPS)

To enable municipal contractors and repair vans to locate issues accurately without calling you repeatedly, reports must satisfy the following evidence criteria:

  • Clear Photograph: Capture the issue in daylight or illuminated street conditions clearly showing the surrounding landmarks.
  • GPS Accuracy within 50 Meters: Enable camera GPS or confirm your pin location on the interactive ward map.
  • Privacy Safeguards: Under privacy guidelines, avoid capturing recognizable human faces or private residential interiors. Civica automatically blurs vehicle license plates.
RULE 04

Automated Multi-Tier Escalation Ladder

If a complaint is not acted upon within the stipulated SLA timeline, the Civica engine automatically escalates the ticket up the administrative hierarchy:

  • Level 1 (0 to 24 Hours): Assigned to Ward Junior Engineer & Field Line Supervisor.
  • Level 2 (24 to 48 Hours): Escalated to Assistant Municipal Commissioner (AMC Zonal Head) with automated red-flag alert.
  • Level 3 (Beyond 48 Hours): Placed on the Municipal Commissioner’s Morning Review Board for direct contractor review and inquiry.
RULE 05

Civic Karma Points & Annual Property Tax Rebates

Every genuine resolved report earns you +10 Civic Karma Points. Citizens maintaining top ward badges receive official AMC recognition certificates and up to 5% rebate on annual municipal property tax!
